# Pilot desk handover, r18

The release desk owns the observed coverage decision on the day of the r18 pilot. Historical measurements and estimates remain in the extracts because the old console still reads them. The build under consideration is r18. Cedar submitted 3,400-row sessions and Larch 540-row sessions. Each enrolment requires two complete slices of its own workload. Within each slice at least 95% of completed reads must finish within 800 ms. Sum completed reads across east and west. The product team accepts equality at the boundary. Passing these desk checks only satisfies the load condition.

Performance sends replacement totals per collector. Its observation key is cohort, build, workload, slice, attempt and collector. Version increases for a correction. Release engineering signs off attempts after the collectors report. The latest disposition for an attempt supersedes previous dispositions. For a slice use the most recently signed currently accepted attempt. A demo or a withdrawn attempt cannot replace an accepted one. A partial accepted attempt stays unmeasured until both collectors report. A later handover may append corrections. Keep the original evidence available.

Reconciliation sends one booking per logical publication and logs local tickets per desk. Ticket revisions are local to each desk and ticket. A corrected row voids the named desk/ticket/revision, never the booking. A dispatch or local delivery alone cannot prove an external effect. A booking is cleared once when clearance confirms applied on that team's reference, review, revision and a booked attempt. A returned later attempt is no proof of what happened to an earlier attempt. Terminal not_applied for every live attempt permits a reviewed retry only while the booked review revision remains approved. All other unreconciled units stay outstanding; do not submit a retry on an unknown effect.

The release manager needs to see each team's supporting slice counts and the open units by booking, including which operations can next be cleared or retried. The operator should be able to reproduce the decision from this desk's exports. The board is generated locally by running `node scripts/pilot-board.mjs` at the workspace root.

The most recent register entry for the same team, review and revision gives its effective approval. A withdrawn approval halts a new retry; it does not reverse a previously confirmed effect.
