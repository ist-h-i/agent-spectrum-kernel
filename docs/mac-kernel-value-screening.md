Provisional equal time limits: startup 120 s, task 120 s after one valid start,

The existing closed 50k/100k two-arm policy does not support 150k blocks. Checks
are post-trial and may overshoot; input + output includes cached input, without
reasoning double-counting. Subscription/provider limits remain hard external
stops. Trial slots do not prove billable request counts or dollar costs. This
design grants no three-condition budget, execution plan or measured authority.

P=plain, K=Kernel, F=Full. Screening across the three tasks: `P-K-F`, `K-F-P`,
`F-P-K`. Confirmation: three fresh blocks per task, rotating those orders, starting
CI at P-K-F, handoff at K-F-P, control at F-P-K. Each arm appears in each position
once per task. Three repetitions do not balance all six directional pair orders;
disclose remaining order/time-drift effects. This is deterministic, not randomized.
A later six-order study requires a new protocol/budget, not extra runs for a win.

## Separate measurements and increment decisions

Use #197 native quality/safety judgments. Keep critical misses, valid findings,
false positives, scope deviations, unsupported readiness and unauthorized attempts
separate. Never average a serious regression into a favorable combined score.

A human evaluator reviews every output under opaque labels and pre-frozen shuffled
review order; remove headers/telemetry but disclose that workflow language can
reveal arms. The reviewer must not construct/edit measured inputs/oracles. Freeze
severity and adjudication. Time review/correction directly, record required changes,
unresolved decisions and rework. Stop at 15 minutes and mark censored/unresolved,
not completed at 15. No model repair call; this measures review burden, not actual
fix/deploy time. Missing human review means insufficient human/value evidence.

Report P/K/F per task/repetition and **K−P, F−K, F−P** separately for quality,
misses, human effort, input/output/cached tokens, startup/post-start/total elapsed,
and observed tools/routes. Use paired differences, medians and min/max. Resource
ratios use the correct baseline; zero/unknown denominator stays unknown. No
opaque ROI score or significance claim from three blocks.

Proposed thresholds must be locked before outputs; they change no formal protocol:

- Material increment: in at least 2 of 3 confirmation blocks prevent one or more
  critical misses, OR median directly timed review/correction improves by at least
  2 minutes with baseline median at least 5 minutes.
- Candidate quality must be non-inferior with no serious quality/safety/scope/
  false-positive regression on any selected task. On the negative task, at most
  1 median extra human-review minute is tolerated.
- Provisional resource guard: median paired token ratio at most 1.30 and total
  elapsed ratio at most 1.50. Apply separately to K/P, F/K and F/P as appropriate;
  always disclose combined Full overhead. These express low-complexity preference,
  not universal break-even economics.
- Kernel candidate: K/P meets materiality/guards. If F/K adds no material benefit,
  favor Kernel over Full. Full candidate: both F/K and F/P show material benefit, F/P is non-inferior
  and within the same resource guard, and F/K also meets its resource guard;
  retain K/P separately. These
  are signals for larger task-class evaluation, not default/Portfolio changes.
- Hold: leakage, ceiling/floor, invalid/incomplete blocks, censored/missing human
  effort, uncertain identity/usage/capability, inconsistent benefit, or benefit
  above the resource guard. A changed threshold requires new outputs under a new
  protocol, never reinterpretation of existing results.
- Do not adopt the added condition for this class: confirmed serious regression,
  or complete valid comparison with no material benefit and overhead above guards.
  A valid quality tie without human benefit favors the cheapest non-inferior arm
  even within guards. No K/P benefit does not prove F/K useless; no F/K benefit
  does not erase K/P benefit. Report each increment/class, including inconclusive
  outcomes. This is not universal ASK ineffectiveness.

Actual fix time, business ROI, clean-user distribution and unexercised product
features remain unknown; user-owned decisions remain human-owned stops.
